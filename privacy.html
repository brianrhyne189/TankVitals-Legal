TankVitals Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026
Developer: Brian Rhyne
Contact: brianrhyne189@gmail.com

TankVitals (“the App”) is an aquarium management application for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Information you provide

You may create and store aquarium-related content in the App, including:

  • Tank profiles (name, size, type, setup details)
  • Fish records (species, names, observations)
  • Water parameter logs and maintenance / feeding schedules
  • Equipment and medication records
  • Photos of tanks or fish that you capture or select
  • Notes and other content you enter

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, Apple may provide a name and email address (or a private relay email) according to the choices you make with Apple. You may also use the App in guest mode without signing in.

iCloud and CloudKit

If you enable iCloud sync, your App data may be stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit so it can sync across your devices and serve as a backup. That data is subject to Apple’s iCloud terms and your Apple ID settings. If you do not enable sync, data remains on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos of fish and tanks (only when you use that feature).
  • Photo Library — to select existing photos (only when you use that feature).
  • Notifications — for feeding reminders, maintenance alerts, and critical tank condition notices (only if you allow notifications).

You can change these permissions in your device Settings at any time.

Network and third-party services

When you search for fish species information, the App may request data from FishBase-related HTTPS APIs. Those requests are for species information and are not used to sell your personal information. Apple services (Sign in with Apple and iCloud) may process data as needed to provide those features.

AI-powered insights

The App may generate suggestions and insights based on your tank data. These are informational only and are not veterinary, medical, or professional advice. You should evaluate recommendations yourself and consult qualified professionals when needed.

How we use information

App data is used to provide aquarium tracking, reminders, sync/backup (if enabled), and in-app insights. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your aquarium data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Data retention, export, and deletion

You can edit or delete records in the App. Export options may be available in Settings. You can also delete the App (and, where applicable, manage iCloud data via your Apple ID). For account or data deletion help, contact brianrhyne189@gmail.com.

Children’s privacy

TankVitals is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. Continued use of the App after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy.
